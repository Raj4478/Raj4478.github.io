Stickman Reels

Small stories, big lessons

Stickman Reels is a small personal tool that makes short Hindi videos in which a stick figure acts out an everyday life lesson, and publishes them on the creator's own Seekh Sketch (सीख स्केच) YouTube and Instagram channel.

What it does

  • Writes a short story about one life lesson and turns it into an animated vertical video.
  • Uploads the finished video as a YouTube Short to the creator's own channel.
  • Reads that channel's own video statistics (views, likes, comments) to learn which lessons people find most useful.

Who uses it

Only the channel's creator. Stickman Reels is not a public service: nobody else signs in, and it never accesses anyone else's account or data.
