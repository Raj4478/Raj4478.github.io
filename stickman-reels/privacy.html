Stickman Reels

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Stickman Reels ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the creator of the Wise Words YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

Google and YouTube data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the channel owner's own Shorts.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the owner's own channel name, list of uploads and their public statistics (views, likes, comment counts) and comments, so the owner gets a weekly summary of how the videos are doing.

The app accesses no other Google data and no data of any other person's account.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to prepare a private weekly summary for the owner.
  • The sign-in token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the app's automated jobs. Statistics are kept only as long as needed to build the summary.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Stickman Reels' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer access the channel, and its stored token stops working.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. For questions, contact the owner through the Wise Words YouTube channel or the GitHub profile.
